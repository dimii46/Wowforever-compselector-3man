                CC Category Guide

                Primary hard CC Reliable control that can remove a dangerous target from combat for a meaningful duration.
                Emergency hard CC Stuns, fears and short incapacitations used to stop immediate danger.
                Reliable interrupt Directly stops spellcasting and usually locks the affected spell school.
                Conditional CC Strong control that only works on certain enemy types or requires a specific pet or situation.
                Soft control Roots, slows and snares useful for positioning and kiting.
                Utility Buffs, dispels, debuffs and group tools that are useful but are not crowd control.
                Important: this is a PvE dungeon synergy model, not a PvP ranking. Talent-dependent and pet-dependent abilities are attached only to relevant profiles or marked as conditional. Final Forever launch tuning may still change individual abilities.
